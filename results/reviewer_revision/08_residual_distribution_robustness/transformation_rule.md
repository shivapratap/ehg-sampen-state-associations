# Transformation decision rule

No transformation was applied. Before inspecting results, a transformed sensitivity was reserved for a feature showing severe conditional-residual shape (absolute skewness at least 2, excess kurtosis at least 10, or at least 2% of residuals beyond three residual SDs) together with a direction disagreement between the frozen mixed model and at least one of the two prespecified robust summaries. No original feature met the combined condition. These cutoffs are diagnostic triggers, not inferential validity tests.
