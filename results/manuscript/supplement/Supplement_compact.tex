\par\smallskip\noindent\footnotesize SampEn fixed-r uses m=2, Chebyshev distance, tolerance 0.2 times the population SD (ddof=0), aligned m/m+1 templates and self-match exclusion. The SampEn profile uses $m=2$ and aligned templates but does not use a single proportional tolerance: its tolerance support is the set of occupied values of the pairwise Chebyshev distances after fixed three-decimal rounding; values are retained only when the shell is observed and both cumulative probabilities exceed $10^{-12}$. Total and average are the sum and mean of retained values; median, SD, kurtosis and skewness use the implementation's population-SD / SciPy bias-default summaries. The rounded GI representation applies the same profile after division of the uncentred input by its population SD. The fixed-grid representation instead evaluates the dimensionless GI profile at a common grid without distance rounding.
